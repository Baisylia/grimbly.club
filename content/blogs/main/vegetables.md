# Vegetables

Vegetables don't exist and I'm tired of pretending that they do. For most of my life I have found it frustrating when people call tomatoes vegetables and not fruits, because they are so obviously fruits! I've always hated the concept of 'culinary fruits' and 'culinary vegetables', because how something is used in cooking does not change what it is. This is still true, but I've changed my mind on tomatoes not being vegetables, because they are actually both. 

The definition of vegetable is an edible part of a plant used as food. This means that every fruit is a vegetable, and vegetable is just a label about edibility rather than the official term for non-fruits we are led to believe it to be. Carrots are roots, pumpkins are fruits, celery is a stem, lettuce is a leaf, and broccoli is a clump of budding florets. Nuts count as vegetables, as do fruits, wheat, and pepper. Interestingly though, mushrooms do not count due to being fungi, so they are the only vegetables that aren't vegetables.

I don't know what to do with this information other than share it with others. I hope you adopt this concept and spread the word.
